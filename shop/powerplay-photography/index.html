Powerplay Photography

Powerplay challenges the myth of the almighty, untouchable Tech Bro by shrinking tech titans down to something unexpectedly ordinary: dolls. t8y created dolls modeled after Mark Zuckerberg, Jeff Bezos, and Elon Musk. This photography is available in an edition of 10 for CHF 180 including the frame, excluding the transportation.
